Features

Made for the way your team actually works

›_

Conversational Queries

Ask questions of your funnel data in plain English. No SQL, no dashboards, no training. If you can type a message, you can query your pipeline.

☎

Mobile-Native

Works inside the WhatsApp app your team already uses. No new login, no app to install, no friction between thinking of a question and getting an answer.

How It Works

From web account to conversation, in four steps

We bring your authorized business data directly into the chat interface your team already uses.

Step 01

Authenticate & Link

Log into your existing VCF web account. Go to your settings and generate a one-time secure link code.

Step 02

Opt-In via WhatsApp

Send that one-time code from your WhatsApp app to our official business number to securely link your device.

Step 03

Ask Your Data

Send a natural text message — like “Show my top deals this week” or “What’s our Q3 funnel looking like?”

Step 04

Get Instant Answers

VCF processes your request and instantly replies with a concise, read-only answer drawn exclusively from the data your account is authorized to see.

Security & Compliance

Built for enterprise security

VCF is designed to protect your data and respect user privacy at every step.

Pillar 01

Read-Only Access

The WhatsApp integration is strictly read-only. Users can query their data, but they cannot alter, delete, or export bulk CRM records via chat.

Pillar 02

Strict Authentication

Only verified phone numbers linked to an active, provisioned VCF tenant account can interact with the system. Unrecognized numbers are ignored.

Pillar 03

User-Initiated Only

We only send messages in direct response to an inbound question from your team, strictly within the standard 24-hour customer service window. We do not send promotional broadcasts.
