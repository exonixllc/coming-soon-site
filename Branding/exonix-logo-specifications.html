01 Primary Lockup

Full Logo

The Exonix™ logo pairs the faceted X-monogram with the EXONIX™ wordmark in a fixed horizontal lockup. The monogram is two-tone: near-black planes with one lighter grey facet that gives the mark its dimensional fold. Never flatten the grey facet or alter the icon-to-wordmark proportion.

Positive — dark on lightReversed — light on dark
02 Icon Component

X-Monogram (Standalone)

The X-monogram is approved for standalone use as an app icon, favicon, avatar, or compact brand stamp where the full wordmark will not fit. It must always retain its two-tone facet structure.
